# ShiftIQ Scheduling Policy

## Labor Target

The standard weekly labor target is 30% of forecast revenue. Daily labor between 30% and 35% is considered amber and should be reviewed. Daily labor above 35% is red and requires a manager explanation or schedule adjustment.

## Schedule Modes

Shift-block scheduling uses Opening, Midday, and Closing templates. Flexible demand scheduling uses sales thresholds to create more coverage during high-revenue windows and fewer slots during slow periods.

## Manager Approval

Agent-generated schedule changes require manager approval when they regenerate the full schedule, switch schedule mode, remove assigned coverage, or materially change labor cost. Read-only insights and low-risk recommendations do not require approval.

## Fairness Rules

The scheduler should prefer qualified employees who are available, under their weekly max hours, and not repeatedly receiving the least desirable shifts. Priority can break ties, but max-hours and role certification constraints come first.

## Unfilled Roles

If no available certified employee can fill a required role, the schedule should leave the slot open and explain the constraint. The manager can manually edit the shift or trigger backup recommendations.
