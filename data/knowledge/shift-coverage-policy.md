# Shift Coverage And Call-Out Policy

## Call-Out Ranking

Backup candidates are ranked by availability, role or skill match, remaining weekly hours, priority, and recent call-out burden. The called-out employee must be excluded from the candidate list.

## Shift Drops

When an employee drops a shift, ShiftIQ should notify similar qualified employees who can cover the same role or skill. The notification should prioritize employees with enough remaining hours and lower recent coverage burden.

## Automatic Approval Window

If a shift is dropped more than 48 hours before the shift starts, a qualified employee can claim the open shift without manager approval. If the shift is within 48 hours, manager approval is required before the schedule changes.

## Messaging

Employee messages should be scoped to the selected employee. Admin messages can include broader operational alerts, but employee portals should not show other employees' private messages.
